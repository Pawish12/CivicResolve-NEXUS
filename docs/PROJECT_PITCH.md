# Project title

**CivicResolve NEXUS — Smart Civic Signal Fusion & Verified Resolution**

## Short submission description (approximately 100 words)

CivicResolve NEXUS transforms isolated civic complaints into accountable community outcomes. Citizens report issues in plain language; a local machine-learning classifier suggests the responsible service department, while an explainable impact score highlights safety risks and waiting time. Similar reports from the same locality can fuse into a shared incident instead of cluttering officials' queues. Officers assign, investigate and document repairs through a protected workspace. Unlike a typical complaint portal, NEXUS asks reporters to **verify the outcome**: a disputed fix immediately reopens the issue. A signal radar and contextual assistant help communities and staff understand emerging patterns and transparent decisions. Built for a hackathon demonstration using fictional sample data.

## One-line pitch

**Cities shouldn't count closed tickets; they should count problems people confirm are fixed.**

## The problem

Disconnected civic reports create duplicate effort and hide community-wide problems. Priority is often opaque, while a status of "resolved" may say nothing about whether citizens see improvement.

## The solution

NEXUS links related civic signals, makes AI-assisted urgency explainable and creates a feedback path from repair evidence to resident-verified outcomes.

## Four differentiators

1. **Conservative civic signal fusion** combines multiple reports into a single incident while preserving multiple citizen observations.
2. **Fairness-aware transparent priority** prevents popularity alone from determining the entire queue (corroboration is capped) and considers age and public safety.
3. **Proof-of-resolution loop** enables citizen confirmation or immediate dispute/reopening of a repair round.
4. **Context-aware civic copilot + warning radar** answers with actual current incident information and surfaces observed complaint clusters.

## What the judges should see

- A polished and responsive civic operations interface.
- Live AI classification as a citizen types a complaint.
- Duplicate-report consolidation tied to matching local context.
- A real persisted officer workflow with access control, evidence and an audit trail.
- A citizen dispute that moves a supposedly completed repair back into the officer's queue.
- A transparent assistant and analytics that react to updated data.

## Limits and honesty

Prototype logic and classifier are trained on small example data; no measured prediction performance, no real emergency decision-making, no verified municipal integration and no full resident identity authentication. These are explicit future work items, not features to overclaim.
