🚀 **Introducing CivicResolve NEXUS — Civic intelligence, verified impact.**

For the **CivicResolve: AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Management and Resolution** challenge at **PromptWars X GDG on Campus PSG iTech (Hack2Skill)**, we built a civic technology prototype with one bold belief:

**A complaint shouldn't count as solved just because someone clicks “Resolved.”**

NEXUS reimagines grievance management as a feedback loop:

🔹 **AI-assisted classification:** Citizen descriptions are mapped to civic service categories using a lightweight local ML model.

🔹 **Explainable impact scoring:** Safety signals, vulnerable locations, report corroboration and waiting time contribute to a transparent, human-reviewable priority.

🔹 **Civic Signal Fusion:** Related reports from the same locality can combine into one shared incident instead of becoming disconnected tickets.

🔹 **Proof-of-Resolution:** Officers document repair work; reporting citizens can confirm the outcome **or dispute the fix, reopening the case**.

🔹 **Contextual CivicGuide + Signal Radar:** A live assistant and an early-warning view help explain decisions and identify clusters of reported problems.

Built with **Python, FastAPI, scikit-learn, SQLAlchemy, SQLite/PostgreSQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript**. Our demonstration uses synthetic sample incidents and is not an official municipal service.

Our vision: **Don't just digitize grievance filing. Make civic resolution transparent, measurable, and accountable to the people experiencing the problem.**
